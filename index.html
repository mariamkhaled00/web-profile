About me

My Name is Mariam, I am 15 years old. I started programming about 4-5 years ago.

Programming is one of the most valuable skills in today’s world. It is the language that allows humans to communicate with computers and create the digital tools, applications, and systems that we use every single day. From websites and mobile apps to artificial intelligence and robotics, programming is the foundation of modern technology. Learning programming is not just about writing code; it is about problem-solving and logical thinking. When you learn to program, you train your mind to break down complex problems into smaller steps, design solutions, and build something useful out of nothing but ideas. It’s a creative process, just like art or writing, but instead of colors or words, you use code. There are many reasons why learning programming is necessary. First, the demand for programmers and software developers is extremely high across all industries. Companies, governments, and organizations need people who can create and maintain digital solutions. By learning programming, you open the door to many career opportunities. Second, programming improves critical thinking and analytical skills, which can help you in many areas of life, even outside technology. Third, it gives you the power to build your own projects—whether that’s a website, a mobile app, or even a small game. As for why you personally chose to learn programming, the reasons can be different for each person. Maybe you wanted to build something new, like your own website or app. Maybe you were curious about how computers really work behind the scenes. Maybe you wanted a career with more opportunities and higher income. Or maybe you just enjoy the challenge of solving problems through code. In short, programming is not only a skill but also a way of thinking. It helps you become more creative, more logical, and more independent. That is why learning programming is so important, and that is why you made a great decision to study it.
